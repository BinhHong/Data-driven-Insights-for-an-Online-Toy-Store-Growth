
# Business Overview

The company is an online toy retailer selling products directly through its website.

Revenue is generated through online product sales.

The dataset captures the customer journey from website visit to refund.

---

Traffic

↓

↓

Pageviews

↓

Orders

↓

Order Items

↓

Refunds

# Stakeholders

Finance

# Business Questions

- Is revenue growing?
- Which traffic source converts best?
- Which products generate the most revenue?
- Which products have the highest refund rate?
- How much revenue is lost through refunds?

# Assumptions

Sessions represent customer acquisition.

Refunds reduce realized revenue.

Products are sold directly through the website.
